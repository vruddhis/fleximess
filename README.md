With the flexi-mess system, students can eat in any mess on campus, but there is no easy way
to decide where to go. Currently, students have to manually check menus of different messes on
InstiApp and have no information about nutrition.
Flexi-Mess Optimiser solves this by scraping daily menus from all messes in one place.
Students can set a preference like high protein, or high fiber, and the app will recommend which
mess is best for them for that meal. It will also provide full nutritional information for all menu
items.
Additionally, students can mark favourite dishes and get notified whenever that dish is served in
any mess. They can also log their meals to track their daily meal intake by choosing the mess
they had food at and recording amount in grams.
